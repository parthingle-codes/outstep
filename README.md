\# OutStep 🌱



\*\*Step out. Experience more.\*\*



OutStep is an open-source AI outdoor companion that generates personalized real-world missions and encourages people to spend less time on their screens.



\## Our approach



\- Open-weight AI for mission generation

\- Mobile-first experience

\- Offline access to saved missions during activities

\- Task timers and manual completion tracking

\- Flexible timing without punishing users for running late

\- Local storage for mission progress and history



\## Status



Under development for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge 2026 — Week 1: Touch Grass.
